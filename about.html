About Toolbelt & Trowel

By the Toolbelt & Trowel Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

Toolbelt & Trowel is an independent workshop journal for keeping a home and yard in good order. It grew from a simple belief: that most people can do far more around their own home than they think, and that the few jobs they should not attempt are easy to recognize once someone points them out honestly.

Our approach: confident, and careful

We try to hold two truths together on every page. The first is encouragement: fixing and building things yourself is satisfying, money-saving, and well within most people's reach when it is explained plainly and patiently. The second is caution: some jobs carry real danger, and knowing when to call a licensed professional is not a failure but a mark of good sense. That is why every repair guide carries a clear "Do it yourself or call a pro?" table — we would rather give you honest guidance than flatter you into a job that could cause harm.

Written with every reader in mind

We write for handy folks of all ages, and with older hands especially in mind. We favor the methods that are kinder to the back, the knees, and the grip, and we say plainly when a job needs strength, reach, or balance that may have become difficult — and when a helping hand or a professional is the wiser choice. Our pages are large, calm, and easy to read, and every guide can be printed for the workbench.

How we write our guides

Every guide lists what you will need, walks through clear steps, and flags the safety points that matter. Our advice is general and errs always on the side of caution, especially around water, height, and anything electrical, gas, or structural, which we do not cover at all. Drafts may be prepared with AI assistance and are reviewed and edited by our editorial team before publishing, and every page carries a "Last reviewed" date.

What we do not do

Toolbelt & Trowel is an independent home-and-yard workshop journal. It is free from top to bottom, sells nothing, and offers no services. We recommend no specific brands; when we name a product type, it is only to teach. Toolbelt & Trowel is an independent educational website and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any company mentioned.

About the photographs

The photographs here are openly licensed images from public collections, downloaded and kept on our own pages rather than borrowed from across the web. Where a license asks for credit, we name the photographer beneath the picture, and a full list lives in the site's image credits file.

Say hello

We love to hear from fellow fixers — a project you tackled, a question that has you stuck, a guide you would like to see, or a correction we ought to make. Reader notes genuinely shape what we write next, and a photograph of something you have built or fixed will make an editor's whole day. Write any time to hello@toolbeltandtrowel.com, and see our Contact page for what to expect.
