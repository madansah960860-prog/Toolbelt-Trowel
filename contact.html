Contact Toolbelt & Trowel

By the Toolbelt & Trowel Editorial Team · Last reviewed: October 2026

We read every message ourselves, and we are always glad to hear from a fellow fixer. The best way to reach us is by email — there is no form to fill in and no account to create.

Write to us

Send your note to hello@toolbeltandtrowel.com. Tell us which guide you were reading and what you were hoping to do, and we will do our best to point you to a clear answer. We aim to reply within two business days.

Spotted a mistake on a page? Our editors welcome corrections at fixes@toolbeltandtrowel.com.

What we can and cannot help with

We are a small team that writes free guides, so there are limits to what an email can do — and some things we will never do.

  • We can point you to the right guide, explain a step more clearly, or fix an error on a page.
  • We cannot diagnose a tricky repair from afar or recommend specific brands or contractors.
  • We offer no paid services and will never ask you for payment. For hands-on help, a trusted local tradesperson is the right call.

A few common questions

Is Toolbelt & Trowel free?
Entirely. Every guide is free to read, there is nothing to buy, and we take no payments and run no advertising.
Why won't you cover electrical or gas work?
Because those jobs carry serious risk and are governed by codes for good reason. They should always go to a licensed professional, and we will not publish steps that might tempt someone into danger.
A job feels too big for me. What should I do?
Call a licensed professional, and feel good about it. Knowing your limits is the single most important handy skill there is.
Will you add more guides?
Yes, over time, and reader suggestions often shape what we cover next. Tell us what you would like to see.

Toolbelt & Trowel
